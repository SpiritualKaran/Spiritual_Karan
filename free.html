Protocol Directives

Non-Negotiable Mechanics

Mānasika Japa Only

All Jaap chanting must be strictly Mansik (Mental). The mantra is repeated entirely within the mind. The lips must not move, and the tongue must remain still. Do not whisper or vocalize.

Digital Hardware

Do not use traditional bead malas for these specific processes. You must use a Digital Finger Counter exclusively to keep an accurate record of your repetitions.
