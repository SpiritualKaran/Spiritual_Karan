Framework

Each Beej Mantra in this practice is assigned a total of 24,00,000 repetitions. There is no daily chanting limitation. The emphasis is on consistency, disciplined practice, and maintaining the same rhythm and speed.

01

Same Seating Place

Keep the designated seating place the same throughout the entire practice.

02

Same Rhythm

Maintain a steady, natural rhythm rather than changing speed from session to session.

03

Mental Japa Only

The prescribed type is Mānasika Japa. Use only a digital counter for counting repetitions.

During the practice, coordinate the chanting with the breath in a comfortable, unforced manner: chant mentally with the natural breathing cycle, keeping the rhythm and speed consistent. Do not strain or force the breath.

Practice Protocol

How to Approach the Japa. The method below preserves the practice principles specified for this five-Beej framework.

Step 01 - 03

Selection & Space

  • 01 / Choose One Beej Work with the selected Beej according to the intended focus: Knowledge, Health, Wealth, Prosperity, or Protection.
  • 02 / Total Count The required total for each Beej Mantra is 24,00,000 repetitions.
  • 03 / Same Seating Place Use the same designated seating place throughout the practice so that the practice environment remains consistent.
Step 04 - 06

Rhythm & Breath

  • 04 / No Daily Limit No daily chanting limitation. Continue according to your available time and ability while maintaining consistency.
  • 05 / Same Rhythm & Speed Keep the chanting rhythm and speed consistent. Comfortable and naturally coordinated with breathing.
  • 06 / Breath Coordination Chant mentally in coordination with the natural breathing cycle without forcing or holding the breath.
Step 07 - 08

Execution

  • 07 / Mānasika Japa Only The prescribed Japa type is mental Japa only. The Beej is repeated internally rather than spoken aloud.
  • 08 / Digital Counter Only Use only a digital counter to record the number of repetitions. Keep the counting method simple and consistent.
