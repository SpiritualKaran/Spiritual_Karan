The Mansik Process

Always perform the Mansik (Mental) Process before commencing any new endeavor, prior to starting your Jaap, or whenever you seek divine connection. If you do not know specific mantras, simply chant the names precisely as written below.

Ganeshay Namah

Kul Dev, Kul Devi Namah

Isht Guru/Dev Namah

Sarv Devi Devta Namah

Pitru Devta Namah

Chant the above sequence 3 times.

Following this, mentally touch the feet of your mother and father to respectfully seek their blessings, and recite:

Gurur Brahmā Gurur Viṣṇu, Gurur Devo Maheśvaraḥ

Guru Sākṣāt Parabrahma, Tasmai Śrī Gurave Namaḥ

Havan for Negativity Removal

Performed for 3 Days (Friday, Saturday, Sunday)

Play Audio on Repeat ▶
The Ritual
  1. Take 7 dry red chilies. Rotate them clockwise 7 times around the afflicted person. Slowly burn each chili in the Havan.
  2. Take another 7 dry chilies. Rotate them anti-clockwise 7 times around the person. Slowly burn each chili.
  3. Alternative: If Havan is not possible, use Kapur (camphor) to burn the chilies.
Quantities & Rules
  • Minor Issues: 7, 11, or 21 chilies
  • Severe Issues: 1.25 kg or more

If extreme fear hits: take 2 incense sticks, rotate clockwise 3 times around yourself, and burn them nearby while praying to your Guru/Shiva.

Crucial Guidelines for Jaap

✦

Hardware: Strictly avoid using any traditional mala. Utilize a digital finger counter exclusively.

✦

The Mansik Method: All Jaap must be Mansik Jaap. This requires the silent, internal mental repetition of the mantra where your lips, tongue, and vocal cords remain absolutely still.

✦

Daily Ritual: Light a pure Desi Cow Ghee Diya in your home temple. Add a small pinch of yellow turmeric powder to the ghee. Maintain the exact same timing for your Jaap daily, and ignite two incense sticks (agarbatti) during the process.

✦

Interruptions: Refrain from visiting the washroom directly during your Jaap. Should the need arise, carefully pause the Jaap, take a sip of water, wait for 5 minutes, and then proceed.

Guru Sankalp Sadhana

41 Days • 1,25,000 Jaap

Light a pure Desi Cow Ghee Diya in your home temple. Hold a few drops of water, a pinch of unbroken rice (Akshat), and a fresh flower in your right hand. Recite the following with deep devotion:

Om gam Ganapataye Namaha, Om gam Ganapataye Namaha, Om gam Ganapataye Namaha

Mera Naam: [Your Name]  |  Mata Ka Naam: [Mother's Name]  |  Gotra: [Your Gotra]
Kul Dev: [Name]  |  Kul Devi: [Name]  |  Isht Guru/Dev: [Name]

O Devo ke Dev Mahadev, Aapko har naam se jaana jaata hai - Bholenath, Shiv Shankar, Shiv Shambhu, Mahadev, Mahakal, Rudra Avatar, Kaal Bhairav, Batuk Bhairav.

Main is deepak ki agni, iski jwala, vayu, aakash, prithvi tatha apne dil, dimaag, aur poore sharir ke har ek ang ko sakshi maankar, aapko apna Guru maanta hoon.

Mantra: Om Na Ma Si Va Ya, Om Na Ma Si Va Ya, Om Na Ma Si Va Ya

Main is mantra ka 1,25,000 jaap 41 dino mein poora karunga. Agar kisi karan se main is jaap ko 41 dino mein poora na kar paun, toh kripya mujhe maaf karein. Main ek naya sankalp lekar phir se shuruwat karunga.

Is mantra ke jaap se mujhe aapka aashirwad mile aur aap mujhe margdarshan dein. Main yeh sankalp leta hoon, jo main aaj se shuru kar raha hoon. Is mantra ki shakti ko koi anya tantra, negative energy, ya manushya kabhi chura na sake.
